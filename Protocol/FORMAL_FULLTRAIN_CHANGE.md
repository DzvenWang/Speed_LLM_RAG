# Formal training policy (v2.0.1)

Formal training is intentionally **balanced**, not full-all-groups.

- Train: 50,000 views total
- 10,000 views per family
- Global `view_group_id` uniqueness
- Val: reuses Development Val exactly (5,000; 1,000/family)

The previous v2.0.0 best-effort all-unique policy produced an imbalanced 56,379-view Formal split and is superseded.
